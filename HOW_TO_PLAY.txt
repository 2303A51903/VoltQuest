========================================================================
BATTERY RUN: THE LOST ENERGY ISLAND - PLAYER CONTROLS & MANUAL
========================================================================

CONTROLS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Move:             W, A, S, D  or  Arrow Keys
- Sprint:           Hold Left Shift (drains energy faster!)
- Jump:             Spacebar
- Look / Camera:    Move Mouse (orbit 3rd-person camera)
- Pause / Menu:     ESC or P
- Interact / UI:    Left Mouse Click

SURVIVAL & ENERGY SYSTEM:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BOLT's battery starts at 100%.
- Power continuously ticks down over time.
- Watch your battery color:
  * GREEN  (60% - 100%): Safe
  * YELLOW (30% - 59%):  Caution
  * RED    (0%  - 29%):  Critical Warning! Alarm will pulse!
- 0% Battery = Shutdown / Game Over!
- Collect normal yellow/green Batteries: +20% power & +100 points.
- Discover hidden glowing purple Crystals: +35% power & +250 points.

LEVEL WALKTHROUGHS & OBJECTIVES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEVEL 1: THE LOST ISLAND
  * Objectives: Collect 5 Batteries & 1 Hidden Crystal.
  * Tips: Follow the sandy trail and cross the stepping stones. Check
    behind the palm grove on the left for the secret crystal!
  * Goal: Reach the glowing beacon at the cliff edge.

LEVEL 2: DANGEROUS JUNGLE
  * Objectives: Collect 8 Batteries & 2 Hidden Crystals.
  * Tips: Time your jumps on the moving wooden platforms. Dodge the rolling
    boulders and avoid the red scanner beams of the patrol drones!
  * Goal: Cross the jungle ravine and reach the ancient gate portal.

LEVEL 3: CRYSTAL CAVE
  * Objectives: Collect 10 Batteries, 3 Crystals & Solve the Color Puzzle.
  * Tips: Look out for trembling stalactites on the cavern ceiling!
  * Puzzle Solution: Step on the colored pedestals in order:
    RED -> BLUE -> GREEN. This lowers the giant crystal barrier!
  * Goal: Reach the glowing shaft at the cavern depths.

LEVEL 4: THE FINAL ENERGY CORE
  * Objectives: Collect 12 Energy Cells & 4 Rare Power Crystals.
  * Tips: Navigate the floating obsidian walkways over the void.
    Avoid sentinel drones and ride moving energy bridges to the central core.
  * Victory: Charge the Grand Core Generator and activate the island beacon!

SCORING & STARS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Normal Battery:       +100 pts
- Rare Crystal:         +250 pts
- Objective Completed:  +300 pts
- Finish Level:         +500 pts
- No Death Bonus:       +200 bonus pts
- High Battery (>50%):  +200 bonus pts

- 1 Star:  Complete the level
- 2 Stars: Collect all batteries and at least 1 crystal
- 3 Stars: Collect all batteries, all crystals, without dying or with >50% energy!

Good luck, Commander! Help BOLT recharge the island and escape!
